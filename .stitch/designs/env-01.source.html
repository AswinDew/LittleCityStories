Honest Transparency Verification

Current Pipeline Status: Native PNG alpha transparency is unsupported by current generation pipeline.

All three master boards are authored on 100% solid, flat, uniform parchment beige (#E7DABC) with generous non-overlapping margins. This enables 1-click chroma keying / alpha mask thresholding in Aseprite or texture packer scripts without edge noise or color fringing.

Honest Seam & Tiling Audit

Seam Status: Ground textures are static art-direction swatches, not seamless runtime tiles.

While internal repetition shows strong organic density and matching palette values, raw edges have micro-pixel boundary steps. Downsampling to 32×32 px with wrap-around offset passes in dedicated pixel software is mandatory before claiming production-ready seamless runtime tiles.
