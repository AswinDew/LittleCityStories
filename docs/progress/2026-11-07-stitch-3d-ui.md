Stitch citizens remain available as source/static previews, not fake work cycles.

Implementation sequence:
1. Publish hash-verified static icons/terrain through a reproducible asset command.
2. Adapt ancient 3D buildings and nature to the Stitch references, retaining shared
   geometry ownership, footprint bounds, entrance/workstation positions and cutaways.
3. Apply the Stitch HUD surface language and actual exported icons to existing controls.
4. Run unit/build/development/production browser checks; inspect rendered screenshots.
5. Fresh review and fixes, then stop at this static integration checkpoint.

No creative image-generation replacement is introduced. Geometry is the approved
technical translation of the frozen Stitch architecture/nature sheets. The legacy
inactive factories retain their existing geometry; shared material colors may be
harmonized, but the ancient catalog is the runtime scope of this increment.

## Delivered

- Eight active ancient building/site models and five environment kinds adapted
  from the frozen Stitch sheets, with timber framing, open entrances, layered
  thatch/wood roofs, crops, log stacks, stone courses and research furniture.
- Existing footprints, research desk coordinates, construction assembly metadata,
  mesh cutaways and shared geometry/material ownership retained. Door/work-area
  rays and height checks cover the new geometry.
- Nineteen native Stitch icons and three terrain textures integrated. The loader
  validates dimensions, palette, binary alpha, source containment and hashes;
  generation rejects missing required art before writing any output.
- Matte parchment HUD, moss selection states, timber borders, warm scene lighting
  and coordinated material colors. Phone layout reserves header/resource rows.
- Existing citizen atlas and its animation tracks retained. No new animation was
  authored. Native 64px Stitch citizen masters remain preview/export sources.
